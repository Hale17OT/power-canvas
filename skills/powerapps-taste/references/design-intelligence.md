# Design intelligence research — 18 September 2026

This is research provenance, not installation or execution of external agent instructions.

## Impeccable

Reviewed the [primary repository](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), its PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md, and the [critique](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/critique.md), [layout](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/layout.md), [typeset](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/typeset.md), [distill](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/distill.md) and [native audit](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/audit.native.md) references.

Adopted: product truth separated from visual memory; page job influences composition; visual judgment precedes mechanical findings; typography uses semantic roles; simplification removes obstacles rather than functionality; focused refinement preserves an established direction. Reports retain strengths and deliberately rejected changes. Native evidence is required after source changes.

Rejected: using a web/CSS detector on Canvas YAML, importing the detector's rule count, converting heuristics into beauty percentages, and applying marketing-oriented display choices to continuous operational work. This implementation is PowerCanvas's own deterministic CLI plus explicit reviewer judgment. It is not an Impeccable command runner or a claim of independent dual-agent assessment.

## DESIGN.md references

Reviewed [VoltAgent's collection](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) and the structure of the [Linear](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/linear.app/DESIGN.md), [Notion](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/notion/DESIGN.md) and [Stripe](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/stripe/DESIGN.md) reference documents.

Common useful structure: atmosphere, semantic color roles, named typography, component treatments, spacing/depth scales, responsive transformations, do/don't rules and known gaps. These are observations about reference sites, often marketing surfaces; they are not official native-app specifications.

The restrained-accent and hairline vocabulary informed the operations direction. Content-led grouping informed the reading direction. A lead measure with supporting comparison informed the decision-brief direction. None imports brand color values, fonts, logos, assets, gradients, typography tracking or exact page layouts. The profiles explicitly reject backdrop blur, custom brand assets and hover-only navigation. CSS grid translates into AutoLayout; hover discovery becomes explicit touchable actions. Native tokens retain the project's established palette and Segoe UI.

The reference importer intentionally requires a reviewed `powercanvas-reference` extraction block. It validates abstraction and adaptation fields; it does not pretend arbitrary marketing prose can be reliably parsed into native controls without judgment.
